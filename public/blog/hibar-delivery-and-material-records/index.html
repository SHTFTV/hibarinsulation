HiBar delivery and material records

By Industry Army Marketing (IAM) · September 28, 2026

Product traceability begins with delivery. A simple receipt and storage routine helps the team identify what reached the site and resolve questions before installation.

Plan the storage arrangement

Ask the supplier and contractor for the current handling and storage requirements. Reserve a suitable location and delivery route. Coordinate quantities with the work phases so supplies do not obstruct exits or other trades.

Check and record deliveries

Keep product names, delivery dates and available batch identification with the job records. Report damaged packaging or questionable condition to the supplier before use. Do not silently substitute a product because it looks similar.

Link records to the work

Associate deliveries and installation dates with the treated areas where practical. Retain the relevant product documents and approved changes. Agree how remaining materials and waste will be managed at completion.

Keep with your project brief

  • Current storage requirements
  • Receipt and product identification
  • Installation and delivery register
