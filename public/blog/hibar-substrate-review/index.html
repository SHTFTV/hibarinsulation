Substrate questions before HiBar application

By Industry Army Marketing (IAM) · September 28, 2026

The surface receiving a spray-applied system is part of the specification. Give the project team an opportunity to review its condition and existing treatments before mobilisation.

Describe what is present

Record the substrate, existing paint or primer where known, and visible contamination or damage. Use photographs linked to drawing locations. Identify unknown coatings as unknown rather than assuming they are compatible.

Request the required preparation

Ask the manufacturer and designer which preparation and compatibility requirements apply to the specified system. Have the contractor explain how those requirements will be met and documented. Surface appearance alone is not an acceptance test.

Set a review point

Schedule the substrate review before application hides the surface. Record areas accepted, outstanding corrections and who authorises progress. If conditions differ from the tender information, resolve the revised scope in writing.

Keep with your project brief

  • Substrate and coating record
  • Preparation requirements
  • Documented release before application
