HiBar: the product and the assembly

By Industry Army Marketing (IAM) · September 28, 2026

Start a HiBar enquiry by identifying the building element and the purpose of the proposed work. The product name alone does not define a complete fire-resistance design.

Identify the material correctly

SOPREMA describes HiBAR as a spray-applied blend of mineral fibres and binders. Do not identify it as polyurethane foam or infer the product from a white, grey or dark finish. Ask for the product documentation and installation records associated with the actual work.

Identify the required assembly

Request the specified assembly and its supporting documentation from the project designer. Record the substrate and building element as well as the intended performance. The designer should confirm how the product is used in that particular arrangement.

Keep the enquiry specific

Send drawings, condition photographs and the project location with your question. Ask which current product documents and assembly details apply. Separate a request for general information from a request to price a fully defined specification.

Keep with your project brief

  • Product identification
  • Specified assembly
  • Project drawings and location
