Discussing the appearance of HiBar

By Industry Army Marketing (IAM) · September 28, 2026

Appearance matters in exposed spaces, but visual choices must remain connected to the specified system. A dark or light finish does not establish material identity or fire resistance.

Describe the visual objective

State the viewing distance, lighting and nearby finishes. Ask whether a sample or representative mock-up is appropriate. Record acceptable texture and variation in plain language with supporting images.

Review proposed finish changes

If a different colour or additional finish is proposed, ask the manufacturer and designer to review compatibility with the actual system. Do not assume a decorative coating can be applied without affecting the accepted specification.

Keep approvals distinct

Record appearance acceptance separately from technical acceptance. Retain the approved sample reference and any conditions. If the installation later needs repair, discuss the expected visual match before work begins.

Keep with your project brief

  • Visual brief and lighting
  • System compatibility review
  • Separate appearance approval
