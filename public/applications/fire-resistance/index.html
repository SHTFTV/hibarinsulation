APPLICATION GUIDE

Begin with the required building assembly.

HiBAR can form part of a fire-resistance design. A coating photograph or product name does not establish a rating for a particular building.

Confirm the design basis

Ask the responsible designer for the specified assembly and applicable documentation. Identify the building element, substrate and required performance.

Plan verification

Agree inspection access, required records and the review of repairs or changes before work starts.
