APPLICATION GUIDE

Describe the sound problem before selecting a system.

HiBAR is used for acoustic control in building assemblies. State whether the concern involves room acoustics, sound transfer or another building requirement.

Explain the space

Describe how the space is used, the surrounding construction and the result the project is seeking. Include plans and existing conditions.

Review the complete construction

Have the designer identify suitable performance evidence for the proposed arrangement. A visible coating alone cannot demonstrate the acoustic result of a whole room or assembly.
