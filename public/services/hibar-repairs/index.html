When to request a repair assessment

Ask for an assessment when overhead work, service alterations or other trades have disturbed an existing coating. Identify each affected location on a drawing and include close and wide photographs taken from an accessible position. Note when the damage appeared and whether its cause has been resolved.

Build a repair brief that can be priced

Include the building address, approximate affected area, height, access restrictions and any product or inspection records. Ask the proposal to identify preparation, compatible material, protection of adjacent areas, cleanup and the records supplied at handover. Separate coating repairs from any firestopping work around penetrations.

Agree how the repair will be reviewed

Have the responsible project team confirm the existing assembly and the repair requirements using current manufacturer documents. The extent of repair should follow the assessed condition, including neighbouring areas, rather than the visible patch alone. Agree inspection access before other trades conceal the work.

Questions about this service

Can damaged HiBAR be repaired?

A repair may be appropriate after the existing product, assembly and cause of damage have been reviewed. The assessment determines whether local repair or wider replacement should be quoted.

What photos help with a repair quote?

Send an overall view showing the location and services, closer views of the damage, and a marked drawing. Include measurements and access details where available.

Is a cosmetic patch enough?

A patch must meet the requirements of the specified system. Appearance alone does not establish the performance of a repaired assembly.
