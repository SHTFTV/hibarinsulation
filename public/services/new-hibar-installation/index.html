Start with the design requirements

Identify the building elements and the thermal, acoustic or fire-resistance objectives in the project documents. HiBAR is a spray-applied mineral-fibre product; the specified assembly and current manufacturer requirements determine how it is used. Provide the relevant drawing revisions and specification sections with the enquiry.

Coordinate access and construction sequence

Describe deck and framing conditions, working heights, lift access and the surrounding services. Ask the project team to agree when substrate review, application, inspection and follow-on trades will occur. Include the work-zone boundaries and any occupancy or scheduling constraints in the quotation brief.

Compare complete installation proposals

Ask each proposal to identify the material, included areas, surface preparation, access equipment, adjacent-area protection, documentation and exclusions. Identify who supplies design information and who reviews the completed work. Retain accepted submittals and location records for future maintenance.

Questions about this service

Is this only for new construction?

A new installation enquiry may concern a new building, an addition or an area newly included in a renovation scope. Describe the existing conditions so the correct work can be assessed.

What documents should I provide?

Provide current drawings, the specification, assembly references, intended performance objectives and available access information.

Can one thickness be used for every project?

Application requirements depend on the specified product and assembly. Confirm them through the responsible project professionals and current manufacturer documents.

Can I ask about thermal and acoustic work?

Yes. State whether the project objective is thermal insulation, acoustic control, fire resistance or a combination so the enquiry can be reviewed against the design.
