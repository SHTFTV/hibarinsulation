Fire spray, insulation and fireproofing

Fire spray and fireproofing are common project-enquiry terms for spray-applied fire-resistive work. HiBAR is a specific mineral-fibre product used in building assemblies for thermal, acoustic and fire-resistance objectives. Identify the required product and assembly when requesting an assessment.

What to include in your enquiry

Send the city, building use, work areas, accessible photographs and available plans. Describe existing conditions, access and timing, and identify whether you need repairs, new installation or complete removal and replacement.

Questions about this service

Which services can I enquire about?

Use the repair, new-installation and complete-replacement pages to prepare your enquiry, then contact PSI through your Lower Mainland city page.

Are fire spray and spray foam the same thing?

They should not be treated as interchangeable. Identify the specified product and purpose of the work; HiBAR is a mineral-fibre and binder system.
