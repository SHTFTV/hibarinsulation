Local preparation for Township of Langley

The Township of Langley publishes separate checklists for accessory buildings, additions and alterations, secondary suites and basement finishing. Its accessory-building information includes a declaration of intended use. Identify whether the project is a dwelling, workshop or another accessory space before asking which insulation system is appropriate.

Confirm these details

  • What is the declared use of the Township building or accessory space?
  • Is the project an alteration, basement finish, suite or accessory-building application?
  • Do the proposed treatment and finished surfaces match the approved use and drawings?

Official local guidance: Residential Building Permits. Checked September 28, 2026. Confirm the route for your exact scope with the municipality.
