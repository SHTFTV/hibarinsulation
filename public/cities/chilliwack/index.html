What should I send with my Chilliwack project enquiry?

Send the building address, use, scope, accessible photographs, available drawings and material records. Describe whether this is new work, damage review or an existing coating assessment.

Can a photograph establish a HiBAR fire rating in Chilliwack?

No. The named product, assembly, installation records and responsible project professionals determine what evidence is required. Do not infer a rating from colour or appearance.
