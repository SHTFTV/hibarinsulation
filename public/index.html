KNOW WHAT YOU ARE SPECIFYING

HiBAR is a mineral-fibre system.

SOPREMA identifies HiBAR as a semi-cementitious, spray-applied blend of mineral fibres and binders. Its intended uses include thermal resistance, acoustic control and fire resistance in building assemblies.

The product name is the beginning of the specification. The actual assembly, substrate and project documents define how it is used.
